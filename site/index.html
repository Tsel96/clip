The canvas

One endless surface. Pan, zoom, and place anything anywhere. Drag files straight from Finder or paste a link — it lands exactly where your cursor is.

Live embeds

Tweets, Instagram and YouTube embed live — videos play right on the canvas. Trim a clip in place without ever leaving the board.

Stacks

Group related cards into a tidy deck with ⌘G. Double-click to fan it open like an album; drag the head to move the whole pile at once.

Colorform & Archive

See your board by colour — CLIP clusters every card into soft clouds of its dominant hue. Or switch to the Archive: everything you've added, newest first, with the exact time you saved it.
