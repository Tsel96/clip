Download for macOS Send links from iPhone Manual

macOS 13+ · Apple silicon & Intel. Open the .dmg and drag CLIP into Applications. It's an unsigned build, so the first launch is blocked: double-click CLIP once, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway → Open. You only do this once — CLIP keeps itself up to date after that.
