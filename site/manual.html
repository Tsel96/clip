Manual

Three views over one canvas, and a phone that feeds it.

⌘1 Canvas

The default free arrangement — an infinite surface for tweets, Instagram and YouTube posts, images, videos, text, sticky notes and drawings. Pan with two fingers, pinch (or ⌘-scroll) to zoom. Add things by pasting a link (⌘V), with Add Post (⌘N), or by dragging files straight from Finder.

Tools: V select, T text, D draw, C connect. While a non-select tool is active, a chip at the top of the canvas shows which one — click it (or press V) to get back.

⌘2 Colorform

Groups cards by their dominant color into clusters and rearranges them on the canvas. Behind the cards, soft blurred color fields melt into an organic constellation; zoom out and the cards crossfade away, leaving just the colors of your board.

⌘3 Archive

A chronological list of everything you've added, newest first, grouped by day — headers with card counts stay pinned while you scroll. Every row shows a preview, a title, the kind of card and the exact time it was added. Click a row to jump to that card on the canvas, selected and framed.

📱 Sending from your iPhone

The pinned “from my iPhone” page is the inbox for your phone. Until something arrives, it shows “Send references from your iPhone” with a setup button. Three steps:

  1. On your iPhone, install the “Add to Canvas” Shortcut — it puts CLIP into your Share sheet.
  2. On your Mac, choose the iCloud Drive folder CLIP should watch (the same folder the Shortcut saves into).
  3. In any app, tap Share → Add to Canvas. The link appears on this page within seconds.
