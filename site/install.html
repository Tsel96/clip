Downloading CLIP…

Your download is starting. Didn’t start? Click here.

Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) · macOS 13 or newer

  1. 1

    Open the file you just downloaded

    In your Downloads, double-click CLIP.dmg. A small window opens showing the CLIP icon next to your Applications folder.

  2. 2

    Drag CLIP into Applications

    Drag the CLIP icon onto the Applications folder in that same window. That installs it. You can now close the window and eject CLIP.dmg.

  3. 3

    Open it the first time

    Because CLIP isn’t from the App Store, macOS checks with you once. In Applications, right-click CLIP → Open, then click Open in the dialog.

    If macOS still blocks it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway → Open. You only ever do this once.

    Still says “CLIP is damaged and can’t be opened”? That’s macOS quarantining an app it can’t verify — clear the quarantine flag once. Open Terminal (Applications → Utilities → Terminal), paste the line below, and press Return:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/CLIP.app

    Then open CLIP normally. (CLIP is ad-hoc signed, not yet notarized by Apple — this is the expected one-time step for indie apps distributed outside the App Store.)
