Installation

  1. 1

    Download

    On your Mac, open clip-umprum.vercel.app and click Download. The file CLIP.dmg will download.

  2. 2

    Install

    Open CLIP.dmg and drag the CLIP icon into the Applications folder.

  3. 3

    First launch

    CLIP was made by an independent developer, so macOS will ask you to confirm once:

    1. Open the Applications folder and double-click CLIP.
    2. A message will appear saying the app can't be opened — click Done.
    3. Open  menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security.
    4. Scroll down to Security. Next to "CLIP was blocked…" → Open Anyway.
    5. Click Open Anyway again and confirm with Touch ID or your password.
